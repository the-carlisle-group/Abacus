DataGrid New Method

Creates a new DataGrid component.

R←{P} New W

P is an optional parent element. W is a namespace (or shortcut list) of properties — see the DataGrid properties. R is the new DataGrid. The data is supplied through the Columns property (or, after creation, with SetColumns).

Examples

      g←A.DataGrid.New''
      g←A.DataGrid.New(ReadOnly:1 ⋄ RowNumbers:1)
